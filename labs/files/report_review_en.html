Gulf Coastal Fisheries Forum · published 14 March 2027

Shira Coast Kingfish Stock Review 2026

Summary

Kingfish landings on the Shira coast fell from 2,840 tonnes in 2023 to 1,960 tonnes in 2026, a decline of 31%.

Sampling at 18 landing sites found that 42% of landed kingfish were below 65 cm fork length, the size at first maturity.

The review was led by Dr. Razeen Al-Musnad, Director of Stock Assessment; he presented it to the Forum on 14 March 2027. Data management was by Ms. Talin Al-Qurayni.

The coast has 1,250 licensed fishing vessels and 3,480 artisanal fishers.

Recommendations

Because 42% of landed kingfish are below the size at first maturity, the Forum recommends:

  1. Close the kingfish fishery for 61 days each year, from 1 September to 31 October, within 12 nautical miles (22.2 km) of the coast.
  2. Vessels may not land kingfish at any landing site during the closed season.
  3. Set the minimum mesh size of gillnets at 100 mm.
  4. Allow bycatch of up to 5 kg per trip, provided it is reported and not sold.
  5. Pay support of SAR 1,500 per month for the two months of the closed season, only to artisanal fishers who held a valid fishing licence on 1 July 2027.
  6. Repeat the stock assessment by 31 December 2028.
